# Validation Plan

A full wide-area IoT disaster recovery deployment should validate:

1. Link budget under terrain, vegetation, weather, and antenna-placement conditions.
2. Gateway coverage with field testing, not only desktop modeling.
3. Battery life under routine, hazard, and emergency reporting modes.
4. Message delivery probability under congestion and degraded backhaul.
5. Alert latency from sensing through local/community notification.
6. Sensor calibration, drift, fouling, and environmental hardening.
7. Gateway power, backhaul redundancy, and store-and-forward behavior.
8. Firmware failure modes, watchdog timers, safe boot, and rollback.
9. Security keys, device authentication, and access control.
10. Local-language alert design and community usability.
11. Maintenance roles, spare parts, replacement batteries, and technician training.
12. Post-event review of missed alerts, false alarms, device failures, and institutional response.

This scaffold uses synthetic data only and should be treated as a reproducible demonstration, not an operational emergency communications design.
