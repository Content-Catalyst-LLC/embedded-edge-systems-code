# Disaster IoT Governance Checklist

## Ownership

- Who owns the sensors?
- Who owns the gateways?
- Who owns the data?
- Who controls historical records?

## Access

- Can local communities see the data?
- Can clinics, shelters, village leaders, and field responders access the alerts?
- Are dashboards available in relevant languages?

## Purpose limitation

- Can disaster data be reused for policing, land control, insurance exclusion, or commercial analytics?
- Is secondary use prohibited or governed?

## Maintenance

- Who replaces batteries?
- Who verifies sensor accuracy?
- Who updates firmware?
- Who repairs gateways?
- Who pays for spare parts?

## Accountability

- Who acts on alerts?
- Who reviews false alarms?
- Who reviews missed alerts?
- Who is responsible when devices fail?

## Equity

- Are remote and historically underserved regions prioritized?
- Are Indigenous or local data sovereignty concerns addressed?
- Are communities treated as system stewards rather than passive data sources?
