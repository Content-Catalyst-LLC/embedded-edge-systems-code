# Ethics and Scope Note

Disaster IoT systems may collect sensitive information about clinics, shelters, roads, bridges, water systems, supply locations, community vulnerability, land access, and infrastructure weakness.

Applied systems should be designed with local governance, data sovereignty, purpose limitation, public accountability, and clear safeguards against surveillance, policing misuse, land control, insurance exclusion, or extractive planning.

This scaffold uses synthetic data for educational purposes. It does not replace community consultation, emergency management planning, telecommunications engineering, humanitarian coordination, or legal review.
