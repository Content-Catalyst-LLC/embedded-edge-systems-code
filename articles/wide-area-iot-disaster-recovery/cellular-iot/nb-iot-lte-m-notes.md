# NB-IoT and LTE-M Notes

NB-IoT and LTE-M can support disaster IoT where cellular infrastructure exists and survives enough to carry low-bitrate telemetry.

NB-IoT is often better suited to static sensors and deep-coverage use cases.

LTE-M is often better suited to mobile assets, logistics, vehicles, and lower-latency applications.

Key planning needs:

- operator coverage confirmation
- SIM provisioning before disaster season
- roaming and fallback strategy
- tower backup power assumptions
- cellular backhaul resilience
- device power profile
- integration with emergency operations systems
