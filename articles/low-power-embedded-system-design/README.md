# Low-Power Embedded System Design

This companion directory supports the article **Low-Power Embedded System Design**.

It treats low-power embedded design as a full energy architecture: energy budgets, duty cycles, sleep-state policies, wake sources, retention, regulator losses, board-level leakage, communications energy, sensor warm-up, brownout recovery, and fleet power observability.

Engineering focus:

- average current and average power modeling
- duty cycle, sleep residency, wake storms, and wake latency
- regulator quiescent current and board-level leakage
- peripheral gating and runtime power management
- radio energy, receive windows, retries, and reconnect cost
- sensing energy and energy per valid measurement
- brownout, persistent state, and low-energy recovery
- fleet telemetry for battery risk and power drift
- systems-code scaffolding in C, C++, Rust, Go, MicroPython, TinyML, PYNQ, and HDL
