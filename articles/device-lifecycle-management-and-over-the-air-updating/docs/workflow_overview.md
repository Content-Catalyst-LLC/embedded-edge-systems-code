# Workflow Overview

This companion directory demonstrates how lifecycle management and OTA updating can be represented through reproducible data, code, and governance evidence.

The workflow begins with device inventory and update-package metadata, checks compatibility and support state, scores OTA readiness in Python, summarizes fleet compliance in R, and stores deployment and lifecycle evidence in SQL-friendly tables.

The systems-language examples show how constrained devices, gateways, and policy validators might reason about update slots, staged rollout rings, rollback readiness, and support-state enforcement.
