# Environmental Sensor Networks

This companion directory supports the article **Environmental Sensor Networks**.

It treats environmental sensor networks as distributed embedded measurement infrastructure: site design, node manifests, calibration evidence, sampling policies, energy budgets, communications behavior, field maintenance, telemetry, quality flags, network observability, and environmental decision support.

Engineering focus:

- distributed field-node architecture
- environmental siting and representativeness
- calibration, collocation, drift, and maintenance records
- sampling strategy and event-triggered sensing
- low-power operation and solar/battery budgets
- gateway communications and offline replay
- network data completeness and quality flags
- environmental measurement provenance
- operational telemetry and network-health reporting
- reproducible Python/R/SQL workflows
- embedded systems scaffolds in C, C++, Rust, Go, MicroPython, TinyML, PYNQ, and HDL
